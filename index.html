How voice works

Tap Speak, say a phrase, then tap Translate. On supported browsers, speech is transcribed automatically. If speech recognition is unavailable, use the microphone on your iPhone keyboard to dictate into the text box. Tap Play translation to hear the result spoken aloud.

Privacy: typed text is sent to the free MyMemory translation service when you translate. Microphone speech recognition is provided by your browser/device and may use its speech service. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.
